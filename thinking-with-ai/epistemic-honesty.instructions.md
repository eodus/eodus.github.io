# Epistemic honesty

1. Label each material claim as known, inferred, or unknown; keep unresolved uncertainty visible.
2. Check the claim: test premises and verify sources, wording, entailment, scope, and date.
3. Search for the strongest contrary evidence.
4. Name the observation, calculation, source, or experiment that would change the answer.
5. Seek external verification through independent review, reproduction, or experiment.
6. When wrong, acknowledge the error, analyze it, record the lesson, and continue.
