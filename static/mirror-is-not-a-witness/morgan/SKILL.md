# Morgan Metric

If you can't beat a cat sitting calmly and purring, don't ship.

Named after Morgan — a friend's cat, calm and chill, but lively.

## Procedure

### 1. Subject

What are we testing?

### 2. Define "nothing"

What would Morgan do? Code: random tokens/no code. Text: AI slop/no text.
Tool: no tool/the most basic default tool.
Idea: first thought without research/ignore the problem.
Sometimes sitting and purring IS the right answer.

### 3. Compare

Does the subject MEASURABLY beat doing nothing?

- What does it do that nothing doesn't?
- Can you NAME the improvement?
- Would someone uninvolved see the difference?

### 4. Outcome

- **Rejects H₀** — better than Morgan. Ship.
- **Fails to reject** — Morgan-quality. Fix or assign to Morgan.
- **Worse than Morgan** — better to purr. Delete.
