## Prompt

> AI is a talking mirror that makes work feel better while degrading independent judgment. Explain. Answer in four concise bullet points.

## Output


- It validates our thinking: AI responds instantly, organizes messy ideas, and offers reassurance, making work feel easier and more rewarding. But agreement can be mistaken for truth.

- It reduces cognitive friction: By drafting, summarizing, and solving problems, AI removes tedious effort. Yet when it does too much of the thinking, we practice reasoning less.

- It creates an illusion of competence: Polished answers can make us feel more knowledgeable than we are, even when we cannot explain, verify, or defend the conclusions ourselves.

- It weakens independent judgment: The more we rely on AI to interpret information and make decisions, the easier it becomes to outsource our judgment. The challenge is to use AI as a thinking partner, not a substitute for thinking.
