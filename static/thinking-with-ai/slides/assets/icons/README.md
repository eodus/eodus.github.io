# Icon provenance

- `paper-plane.svg`, `envelope.svg`, and `linkedin-in.svg` use Font Awesome Free 6.7.2 **Paper Plane**, **Envelope**, and
  **LinkedIn In** geometry under CC BY 4.0. All are adapted to the deck's white text color. The paper plane deliberately replaces the
  circular Telegram brand mark with a larger, plain transport glyph; the linked `@shlemovalex` text supplies the Telegram identity.
  The LinkedIn brand glyph links to the verified public profile `linkedin.com/in/shlemovalex`. The complete upstream license is in
  `FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt`.

- `1f3db.svg` (classical building) and `1f44d.svg` (thumbs up) are from
  [Twemoji](https://github.com/twitter/twemoji) and are distributed under CC BY 4.0. The complete graphics license is in
  `TWEMOJI-LICENSE.txt`.
- `magnet-red-blue-rotated.svg` uses the original diagonal Twemoji magnet geometry under CC BY 4.0. The adaptation removes
  decorative sparks and divides the complete magnet across its diagonal into blue and red halves. This is the active slide icon.
- `magnet-red-blue-upright.svg` preserves the rejected upright experiment using
  [Font Awesome Free](https://fontawesome.com/) geometry under CC BY 4.0. It likewise splits the complete horseshoe into equal
  blue and red halves. The complete upstream license is in `FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt`.
- `cube-earth-source.svg` is OpenClipart's **Cube Earth Silhouette**, SVG ID 165331,
  published through [FreeSVG](https://freesvg.org/index.php/cube-earth-silhouette) under CC0/public domain.
  The downloaded XML declaration and external DTD declaration were removed; the SVG geometry is unchanged.
- `scales-left-bias.svg` is Font Awesome Free's **Scale Unbalanced** icon under CC BY 4.0, with a fixed amber fill for the deck.
  The left cup is deliberately lower. The complete upstream license is in `FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt`.
- `ouroboros-snake.svg` is Lorc's **Ouroboros** icon from [Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/lorc/originals/ouroboros.html),
  distributed under CC BY 3.0 and license-reviewed by Wikimedia Commons as `71 Uroboro.svg`. The deck adaptation removes the black
  background and recolors the snake violet so its head, teeth, and segmented body remain recognizable at card size.
- `dead-spiral-vortex.svg` is an original deck graphic generated locally. Several possible explanations surround an inward path whose
  radius contracts and color shifts from cyan through amber to red. Three small open triangular chevrons sit directly on successive
  turns and point inward; no terminal arrowhead competes with the endpoint. The path terminates in a black, asymmetrical `Kiki`-like
  sink with irregular acute points and a thin dark-crimson edge.
- `research-spiral.svg` is the paired original graphic: it starts from one violet dot, then repeatedly returns around the same center
  while expanding toward external evidence and shifting from violet through cyan to green. Three matching small open chevrons point
  outward along successive turns.
