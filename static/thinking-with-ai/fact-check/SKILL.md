# Fact-check

Input: an artifact and its intended use.

1. Separate material facts, inferences, opinions, and quotations.
2. For each factual claim, verify the source, wording, entailment, scope, and date.
3. Search for the strongest contrary evidence.
4. Mark the claim `verified`, `contradicted`, `mixed`, or `unsupported`.
5. Preserve uncertainty and propose the smallest accurate correction.

Output: a claim ledger with verdict, evidence, contrary evidence, and correction.
