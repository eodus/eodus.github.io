  <div class="register-card falsifier fragment" data-fragment-index="5"><strong>DESIGN A FALSIFIER / UNIT TEST</strong><span>What could change that position? Make the answer executable</span></div>

  <div class="register-card morgan fragment" data-fragment-index="7"><strong>MORGAN TEST</strong><span>Give the questionable idea one fair chance to beat nothing, random, or a purring cat</span></div>
</div>

<p class="bottom-line fragment" data-fragment-index="8">Inspect the evidence · own the result</p>

## Preserve the operation being learned

<p class="slide-subtitle register-generation-claim">Despite its name, <em>generative AI</em> does not have to generate the whole artifact at once</p>

<div class="register-grid practical learning-operations">
  <div class="register-card quiz"><strong>QUIZ ME</strong><span>Make a practice test; hide answers until I commit</span></div>
  <div class="register-card hint"><strong>GIVE ME A HINT</strong><span>Preserve the next reasoning step instead of solving it</span></div>
  <div class="register-card pupil"><strong>LET ME TEACH YOU</strong><span>Ask questions until my explanation exposes its gaps</span></div>
  <div class="register-card check"><strong>PRACTISE MY ERRORS</strong><span>Generate exercises from recurrent mistakes, not generic drills</span></div>
  <div class="register-card factcheck"><strong>EXPLAIN, THEN TEST</strong><span>Require transfer to a new case rather than fluent repetition</span></div>
  <div class="register-card surprise"><strong>REMOVE THE AI</strong><span>Test delayed unaided performance on the target operation</span></div>
</div>

<p class="bottom-line learning-bottom-line"><strong>ASK × 6 ⟶ GENERATE × 1</strong><span>Do not let artifact generation replace the practice that the student needs</span></p>

<p class="eyebrow">FISHER · A NULL MODEL IN EIGHT CUPS</p>

## Blind testing makes sense for AI

<div class="tea-experiment">
  <div class="tea-claim"><strong>THE CLAIM</strong><span><em>&mdash; I can distinguish which was added to the cup first: milk or tea.</em></span></div>
  <div class="tea-cups-group">
    <div class="tea-cups" aria-label="Eight cups: four milk-first and four tea-first, presented in random order">
      <i class="cup milk">M</i><i class="cup tea">T</i><i class="cup tea">T</i><i class="cup milk">M</i>
      <i class="cup tea">T</i><i class="cup milk">M</i><i class="cup milk">M</i><i class="cup tea">T</i>
    </div>
    <p><b class="milk-key">M</b> milk first · <b class="tea-key">T</b> tea first · labels hidden from the subject</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tea-design"><strong>THE DESIGN</strong><span>8 cups · 4 each · random order · condition hidden · classify all eight</span></div>
</div>

<div class="tea-result"><span>P(8/8 correct | no discrimination) =</span><strong>1 / 70</strong></div>

<p class="source-case-reference bottom-reference"><a href="https://web.ma.utexas.edu/users/mcudina/m358k-lady-tasting-tea-original.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><em>R. A. Fisher · The Design of Experiments · ch. II · 1935</em></a></p>

<p class="bottom-line">Be creative in the design. Be practical in implementation. Be rigorous in the decision rule</p>
